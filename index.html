Bring in the video's subtitles

With subtitles, sentences can be cut automatically and each sentence shows its text.

  1. Once: drag this button to the Chrome bookmarks bar (⌘⇧B shows the bar). ⇢ Send to Shadowing
  2. Open the video on youtube.com in Chrome and click the bookmark.
  3. This page opens the video with its subtitles. Press ✂ Auto-cut to make the sentences.

Settings

On iPhone, if a button doesn't start the video the first time, tap the video once. iPhone needs one direct tap before buttons can control it. On a Mac: Space play/pause (pausing saves a sentence), R repeat, ← → previous/next, V record, Enter +1 rep, G grab (Grab mode).
